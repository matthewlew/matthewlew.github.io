Approach

House rules.

02 / 04

There is no such thing as a perfect design system. Startups burn months custom-building every component before they know what the product needs.

So I run the 80/90 rule: take a standard library off the shelf to cover 80–90% of the screens on day one. That buys the team momentum, and it leaves designers room to solve the genuinely unusual cases without fighting a rule.

  • Drift is a signal, not a violation When designers diverge from the system, the product is telling you it moved. The job is to absorb the good patterns back, not to police the file.
  • Figma variables map 1:1 to code Aligned with lead engineers early, so a handoff is a shared foundation rather than a translation exercise.
  • The system is the context layer Give an agent clear token rules and it emits production UI. Give it none and it emits inconsistency at speed.
  • Managing risk, not files As products grow, everything is connected. The work is making sure the system can evolve without breaking three platforms at once.
