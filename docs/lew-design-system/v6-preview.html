LDS v6 — overlays and data display

The v6 spec, rendered. Every panel below paints from LDS tokens only — no hex, no font family — so this page cannot quietly disagree with the system it is proposing against. All four shipped. The live components now live in lds.css and are demonstrated in the showcase — that page is the source of truth. This one is kept as the record of the proposal and its reasoning; where the two disagree, the showcase is right.

1 · The size scale is derived, not picked

--modal-lg is the anchor. At --text-body 18px, 560px is about 62 characters — the top of the comfortable measure band. Everything at or below it is one column of prose; everything above it is not a column of prose at all.

The seam sits at lg. That is why xl and 2xl are separate steps rather than degrees of “bigger”, and it is what stops someone adding a 640 later because it looked right.

Size is declared, never measured from content. A modal that resizes as its own content settles — one width loading, another loaded — reads as broken. Stability beats fit. The named class sets only --modal-w, so --modal-w:640px inline stays available as the designer's lever.

Alignment falls out of the docking rule

Centred when the overlay interrupts. Left when it accompanies. Centring is a three-column grid, not text-align — with text-align the title shifts sideways the moment a back button appears, which is what makes nested flows feel unstable.

4 · Nesting is a stack, not a second overlay

Back left, close right, never the same target. Back pops one level; close dismisses the whole stack. A scrim tap closes everything — the user tapped outside all of it, not on the parent panel, and popping one level would mean an outside tap behaves differently at a depth they cannot see.

6 · List row — the component neither system has

Three regions: leading, content, trailing, plus an optional affordance. The content region gets min-width:0, without which the subtitle refuses to truncate and shoves the trailing region off the row — the same min-width:auto trap already documented on the banner.
